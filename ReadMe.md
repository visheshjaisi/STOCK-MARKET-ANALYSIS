    "# Stock Market & Portfolio Analysis\n",
    "\n",
    "## Overview\n",
    "\n",
    "This project analyses the historical performance and risk of Apple (AAPL)\n",
    "\n",
    "and Nvidia (NVDA) using Python. It investigates returns, volatility,\n",
    "\n",
    "correlation and risk-adjusted performance, before constructing a 50/50\n",
    "\n",
    "portfolio of the two stocks.\n",
    "\n",
    "## Objectives\n",
    "\n",
    "- Analyse historical stock returns\n",
    "\n",
    "- Measure and compare volatility\n",
    "\n",
    "- Investigate the correlation between Apple and Nvidia\n",
    "\n",
    "- Construct a diversified 50/50 portfolio\n",
    "\n",
    "- Compare portfolio performance with the individual stocks\n",
    "\n",
    "- Evaluate risk-adjusted returns using the Sharpe ratio\n",
    "\n",
    "## Technologies\n",
    "\n",
    "- Python\n",
    "\n",
    "- Pandas\n",
    "\n",
    "- NumPy\n",
    "\n",
    "- Matplotlib\n",
    "\n",
    "- yfinance\n",
    "\n",
    "- Jupyter Notebook\n",
    "\n",
    "## Methodology\n",
    "\n",
    "Daily returns were calculated using percentage changes in closing prices.\n",
    "\n",
    "Annualised volatility was calculated using the standard deviation of daily\n",
    "\n",
    "returns multiplied by √252.\n",
    "\n",
    "The portfolio consisted of a 50% allocation to Apple and a 50% allocation\n",
    "\n",
    "to Nvidia.\n",
    "\n",
    "The Sharpe ratio was calculated using a risk-free rate of 3.75%.\n",
    "\n",
    "## Results\n",

    "| Statistic | Apple | Nvidia | Portfolio |\n",

    "|---|---:|---:|---:|\n",

    "\n",
    "| Annual Volatility | 25.02% | 36.87% | 23.45% |\n",
    "\n",
    "| Sharpe Ratio | 1.25 | 0.47 | 1.04 |\n",

    "\n",
    "## Conclusion\n",
    "\n",
    "The analysis demonstrates the potential benefits of diversification.\n",
    "\n",
    "Although Apple produced the highest total return and Sharpe ratio,\n",
    "\n",
    "the 50/50 portfolio had the lowest annual volatility.\n",
    "\n",
    "The relatively low correlation between Apple and Nvidia contributed to\n",
    "\n",
    "the reduction in portfolio risk.\n",
